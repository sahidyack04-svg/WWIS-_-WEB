Admission

Three Simple Steps to Join WWIS

Home / Find Us / Admission

Step 1 · Plan Your Visit

Schedule a one-on-one session with our academic staff to learn about our curriculum, teaching approach, facilitating methods, and campus facilities.

Step 2 · Get to Know Your Child Better

We meet with your child directly to understand their background and learning needs — the first step toward building their Individual Learning Plan (ILP).

Step 3 · Submit Your Application

Submit the required documentation and your completed application form within a week of your discussion with us to confirm your child's seat.

Ready to begin?

Kindly fill out our enquiry form and our admissions team will get in touch with you to schedule your visit.
