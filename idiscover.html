iDiscover

Focuses on discovering unique skills, passion and strength.

iDiscover is a future-focused programme that bridges foundational learning with real-world application — combining academic rigour, skill development and strong community values. It moves beyond memorization, preparing learners to think independently, collaborate effectively, and adapt to a rapidly changing world.

24Projects
8Professional Skills

Real-World Learning, Grade-Level Growth

Assessment is conducted based on grade-level progress rather than marks, reducing academic pressure while maintaining standards. Internships, field visits and industrial visits connect classroom concepts to real-world contexts.

Languages, Community & Confidence

Language Hours cover Tamil, English, Mathematics and Hindi, supported by workbooks and continuous practice. Weekly community meetings give students space for reflection, expressing opinions, and building emotional intelligence and confidence in communication.

Student-Led Leadership

Through their Individual Learning Plan, students explore career-oriented skills such as programming, graphic designing, culinary arts and visual arts while building a portfolio. Students manage school events entirely — agenda, technical execution, coordination and snacks — building leadership, responsibility and teamwork.
