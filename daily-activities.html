Daily Activities of Your Child

Education, World-Class

Every Day, Built With Purpose

Education of world-class quality that meets global standards and shapes future-ready students. Every day at WWIS blends structured learning with time for exploration, creativity and reflection.

ILP for Discovering Strength

A specific strategy that helps us identify each child's strengths and weaknesses — unlike the regular pattern followed elsewhere — so we can match skills and interests to future career goals.

Independent Time for Decision Making

Children engage in activities autonomously within an exploratory environment, fostering lifelong curiosity and creative thinking for unfamiliar situations.

Creative Challenge for Creative Confidence

Creative thinking is not a talent — it's a skill that can be learned. It empowers children by adding strength to their natural abilities.

Exploration Hour

Learning mathematics and science by connecting new concepts with existing knowledge through active participation and observation-based questioning.

Language Hour

Tamil, Hindi and English are introduced through storytelling, circle time, songs, rhymes and discussions — progressing through listening, speaking, reading and writing.

Smart Moves for Brain Development

Personalized physical activity develops motor skills and interpersonal abilities, enhancing memory, concentration and focus.

Nature Time

Garden lessons and nature walks support emotional development, reduce screen time, and help children understand their role in the natural world.

PBL for Real World Skills

Project-based learning engages students in solving real-world problems over two weeks, building critical thinking, collaboration, creativity and communication.

IECP for Career Planning

The Individual Exploration Career Programme helps students discover interests and explore career options through guided activities and research.

Every activity ties back to one Individual Learning Plan
