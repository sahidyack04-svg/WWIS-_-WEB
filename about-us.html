Our Teaching Methodology

Home / About Us

Child Centric Learning

The school operates with a child-centric philosophy where every decision prioritizes learner needs. The approach provides children with "time, space, and opportunities they need to develop essential skills at their own pace."

  • ✓ Open learning spaces encouraging exploration
  • ✓ Spacious classrooms designed for creativity
  • ✓ Multiple learning methods for different styles
  • ✓ Facilitators who listen to children
  • ✓ Curriculum based on learners' everyday experiences

Child-Led Learning

Students are encouraged to take responsibility for their own learning through choice and planning.

  • ✓ Learners deciding daily learning plans and methods
  • ✓ Students planning and conducting events
  • ✓ Supporting learners in creating community rules

Focus on Individual Learning Plan

The school recognizes each child's unique strengths through personalized guidance and support.

  • ✓ Celebrating each child's uniqueness
  • ✓ Helping learners discover strengths and patience
  • ✓ Setting and monitoring goals
  • ✓ Dedicated learning plans for each student
  • ✓ Regular progress reviews by coordinators

Build Self-Direction and Leadership Skills

The school nurtures self-directed learners by encouraging responsibility and reflective thinking. Students develop:

  • ✓ Goal-setting and learning management abilities
  • ✓ Responsibility and accountability
  • ✓ Leadership and teamwork skills
  • ✓ Effective communication and decision-making

Focus on Real World Learning

The school emphasizes connecting classroom knowledge to everyday life through practical experiences, problem-solving activities, and meaningful projects. Students engage in:

  • ✓ Applying learning to real-life situations
  • ✓ Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • ✓ Hands-on projects and activities
  • ✓ Collaboration and communication
