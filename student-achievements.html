Big or Small, Every Win Counts

Big or small, every student achievement is a win worth celebrating. Here's a glimpse of what our learners have created this year.

Story Books

Our learners didn't just read stories — they became published authors, exploring themes from the mysteries of the sun to space adventures and wisdom tales, each book a product of bold, original thinking.

Culinary Arts

From fresh fruit juices and nutritious sandwiches to momos and other wholesome snacks, learners explored food preparation while building confidence, hygiene awareness and practical life skills.

Art and Craft

Blank canvases transformed into bold, vibrant mural art — each piece reflecting patience, precision and a strong artistic vision built through color, detail and meaning.

Website

Using Python, our learners designed and developed functional websites — from planning layouts to implementing backend logic — gaining hands-on experience turning ideas into working digital solutions.

Product Designing

Hands-on prototyping produced functional truck models, Tangram puzzle sets for spatial thinking, and interactive games — applying creativity, engineering logic and usability in thoughtful, well-executed outcomes.
